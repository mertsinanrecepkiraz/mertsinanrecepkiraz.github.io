# Research

<div class="research-grid">

<div class="research-card">
<div class="research-body">
<h2 class="research-title">Marchenko Methods</h2>
<p class="research-desc">Data-driven redatuming and focusing methods that reconstruct wavefields at virtual locations in the subsurface while accounting for multiple scattering. This work studies practical requirements such as velocity-model accuracy and wavefield decomposition.</p>
</div>
</div>

<div class="research-card">
<div class="research-body">
<h2 class="research-title">Seismic Processing with Machine Learning</h2>
<p class="research-desc">Physics-informed and data-driven approaches for suppressing free-surface multiples and ghost reflections. Trace-by-trace convolutional neural networks offer a path toward robust processing when acquisition geometries are sparse or irregular.</p>
</div>
</div>

<div class="research-card">
<div class="research-body">
<h2 class="research-title">Wave Propagation & Imaging</h2>
<p class="research-desc">Computational seismology, seismic interferometry, and Green's-function retrieval for improved imaging of complex subsurface structure. The emphasis is on understanding how recorded wavefields can be transformed into reliable images.</p>
</div>
</div>

<div class="research-card">
<div class="research-body">
<h2 class="research-title">Subsurface Monitoring</h2>
<p class="research-desc">Seismic redatuming and imaging for monitoring injected CO<sub>2</sub> and other changes near a target reservoir. This work includes applications to the Frio carbon sequestration experiment using synthetic and observed vertical seismic profile data.</p>
</div>
</div>

</div>
